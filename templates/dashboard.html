What Does Corporate Payroll Do?

Payroll is responsible for ensuring all Global Payments employees are paid accurately and on time. We manage and process pay, from approved time reporting to final paycheck delivery, while ensuring compliance with tax and legal requirements.

How Payroll Can Help You:

We're available to assist with questions related to:

  • Payroll Calendars & Paystubs: Understanding pay dates and reviewing your pay statements.
  • Gross-to-Net Pay Processing: Ensuring your earnings, statutory contributions, and deductions are calculated correctly.
  • Approved Time & Payments: Processing EIS-approved hours, leaves, night diff, rice subsidies, bonuses, and other pay elements.
