What Does Corporate Payroll Do?

Payroll is responsible for ensuring all employees are paid accurately and on time. We manage and process pay, from approved time reporting to final paycheck delivery, while ensuring compliance with tax and legal requirements.

How Payroll Can Help You:

We're available to assist with questions related to:

  • Payroll Calendars & Paystubs: Understanding pay dates and reviewing your pay statements.
  • Gross-to-Net Pay Processing: Ensuring your earnings, taxes, and deductions are calculated correctly.
  • Approved Time & Payments: Processing EIS-approved hours, leaves, bonuses, and other pay elements.
  • Pay Calculations & Reconciliation: Verifying your pay reflects all approved time and payment items.
  • Garnishments & Wage Withholding: Managing court-ordered deductions from your pay.
  • Direct Deposit: Ensuring your paycheck is deposited correctly into your bank account.
  • Tax Deductions & Payments: Withholding and remitting payroll taxes in compliance with regulations.
